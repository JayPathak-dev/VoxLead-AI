VOXLEAD SOLUTIONS - IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINES
============================================

Phase 1: Discovery & Knowledge Audit (Days 1–3)
- Understand business goals, daily inquiry volume, target customer personas, and sales qualification criteria.
- Collect product brochures, pricing sheets, FAQs, and policy documents.

Phase 2: Knowledge Indexing & Agent Configuration (Days 4–7)
- Clean, chunk, and index documents into the FAISS vector database.
- Configure brand tone, English/Hindi prompts, lead scoring weights, and appointment slot durations.

Phase 3: CRM & Workflow Testing (Week 2)
- Validate end-to-end lead extraction, sentiment tagging, double-booking prevention, and call summarization.
- Conduct internal UAT (User Acceptance Testing) with your sales team.

Phase 4: Go-Live & Optimization (Week 3+)
- Launch the AI agent on your web portal or voice workflow.
- Monitor conversion rates, common customer objections, and average lead scores via the Analytics dashboard.
