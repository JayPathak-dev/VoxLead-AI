VOXLEAD SOLUTIONS - CORE SERVICES
=================================

1. AI CHATBOT SERVICES (Omnichannel Web & WhatsApp Assistants)
- Custom RAG-powered AI chatbots trained on your product catalogs, PDFs, FAQs, and policies.
- Handles order tracking inquiries, return/refund policy questions, product recommendations, and troubleshooting.
- Multilingual support in English and Hindi (Hinglish-friendly understanding).
- Seamless handoff to human sales or support agents with full conversation summary.

2. VOICE AUTOMATION (Autonomous AI Voice Sales & Booking Agents)
- Browser and telephony-ready AI voice assistants capable of inbound qualification and outbound follow-ups.

- Extracts caller name, company, business requirement, timeline, and budget automatically.
- Checks real-time slot availability and books sales discovery calls or service appointments after explicit confirmation.

- Automated follow-up task generation, pipeline status updates (New, Contacted, Qualified, Unqualified, Converted), and analytics dashboards.
